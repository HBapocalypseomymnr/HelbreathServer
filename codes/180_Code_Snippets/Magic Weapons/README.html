I used a new special attribute for the magic weapons, as the critical, ancient, poisionning or righteous weapons. The new weapon's class is now "Magic". I added 16 different Spell Effects (from Fire-Strike to Earth-Shock-Wave) and 15 damage levels (from 7% to 105%).

How does it work ?
You just have to attack an ennemy with the magic weapon, the HG will calculate the % of damage added to the regular weapon's damages and show the spell effect. It works with one handed and two handed weapons.

Does it always work ?
Of course no ! MP are decreased each time you attack an ennemy, and if you don't have enough hp to cast magical attack, the weapon throw a regular attack on the ennemy, without magic effect, and without and damage bonus. When MP have been regenerated magic attacks are back again !

How can i calculate the item attribute for these magic weapons ?
Use the tool provided with the code... it's an ugly console application but i didn't have time to code anything better for now, and atleast it works !

How do i get Magic Weapons ?
Drops have been edited ! Now you can have magic weapons when you kill ennemies. Many checks will allow peoples who kills strong monsters to have better weapons. ie : If you kill an Ogre, it will NEVER drop a weapon with less than 63% of damage added, and spell will be at least Ice-Whirlwind. A Demon will drop atleast a 70% damage weapon with atleast Meteor-Strike !! You can also summon magic weapons with a GM char, using the tool provided.
