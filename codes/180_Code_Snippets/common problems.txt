?:Enyone know how to calculate weapon damage when u have damage
"4D8+3 with 300str" or "2D11+9 with 400str" etc

?:how is spells calculated?

A:Lets say...the weapon damage is 4D8+3 and needs 300str
Lowest Damage
4D1+1 = 5 points.
Now 300str/10 = 30% extra damage.
A 30% of 5 points will be... 1.5 so the lowest damage should be
5 points + 1.5 points = 6.5 points <--- very rarely

Highest Damage
4D8+3 = 35 points
Now 300str/10 = 30% extra damage.
A 30% of 35 points will be... 10.5 so the highest damage should be
35 points + 10.5 points = 45.5 points

This is no dash, no zerk or critical. Just normal hit.
By my calculations, the normal damage should be around 30s
